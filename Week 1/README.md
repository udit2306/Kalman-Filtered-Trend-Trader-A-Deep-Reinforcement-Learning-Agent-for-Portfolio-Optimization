# Week 1 – Foundations of Machine Learning

This folder contains **Week 1 WiDS assignments**, focusing on the **mathematical and statistical foundations** of machine learning models.

The emphasis is on **theory, derivations, and intuition**, forming the backbone for later applications in filtering and trading systems.

---

## Topics Covered

### 1️ Linear Regression
- Scalar and matrix formulation
- Ordinary Least Squares (OLS)
- Mean Squared Error (MSE)
- Normal equations
- Conditions for invertibility

### 2️ Optimization
- Gradient descent (batch)
- Learning rate interpretation
- Convergence behavior

### 3️ Bias–Variance Decomposition
- Formal derivation
- Interpretation of bias², variance, and irreducible noise
- Effect of model complexity

### 4️ Multicollinearity
- Omitted variable bias
- Condition number of \( X^T X \)
- Why variance increases without introducing bias
- Stability vs unbiasedness

- **Notebook**: Implementation-based questions and experiments

## Learning Outcome

By the end of Week 1, I developed:
- Strong intuition behind regression models
- Understanding of **why models fail**
- Ability to diagnose instability and bias
- Mathematical rigor required for advanced ML systems

This theoretical grounding is essential for understanding **Kalman Filters and adaptive models** in later weeks.
